# Sources checked (searched online on 2026-11-19)

- Great Pyramid of Giza completed c. 2560 BC (sources agree on roughly 2580-2560 BC)
- Cleopatra VII lived 69-30 BC
- Apollo 11 Moon landing: 20 July 1969
- Gaps: pyramid to Cleopatra about 2,500 years; Cleopatra to Moon landing about 2,000 years. Both are rounded in the reel.
