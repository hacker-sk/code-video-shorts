# Notes: octopus-three-hearts

## Sources checked 2026-11-24
- Smithsonian Magazine, Ten Wild Facts About Octopuses: two hearts move blood past the gills, the third circulates oxygen-rich blood to organs and muscles; copper in octopus blood makes it blue
- PBS Nature, Octopus: three hearts (two through the gills, one through the body), blue blood with copper-based hemocyanin
- Live Science, How many hearts does an octopus have?: systemic heart pumps blood around the body, two branchial (gill) hearts
