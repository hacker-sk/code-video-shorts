# Scene design: octopus-three-hearts (vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps)

Background: deep-sea gradient (#06283d top to #02101c bottom), slow light rays, rising bubbles. Fade in from black over 0.4 s. Small label WILD FACTS at the top (y 150).

Character: flat cartoon octopus (orange #ff8a5c, darker spots), big white eyes with moving pupils, 8 wavy tentacles drawn as tapering circles, centred at (540, 900) scale 1.5 with a slow bob. Hearts drawn inside the head: two gill hearts at the sides (x -120 and +120) and one body heart in the middle, beating.

Captions: bold 74px white with dark outline, bottom area (y about 1600), one ALL-CAPS word per line highlighted in yellow (HEARTS, BLUE), fade in per line (same style as freeform-sharks-trees).

Lines (timing follows the voice, window.__TIMING, else 0.4 s + 0.3 s per word):
1. Octopuses have three HEARTS: big number 3 at top; the three red hearts pop in one after another and beat.
2. Two feed the gills: top text 2 and GILL HEARTS; body heart dimmed; red dots flow from the two side hearts out to little gill fans.
3. One feeds the body: top text 1 and BODY HEART; side hearts dimmed; red dots flow from the middle heart down the tentacles.
4. And their blood is BLUE: hearts and dots fade from red to blue, blue glow around the octopus; top text BLUE BLOOD and COPPER, NOT IRON.
5. Follow for more wild facts: octopus bobbing, pulsing yellow FOLLOW button.
